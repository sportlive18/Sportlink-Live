Afghanistan vs Ireland – 1st ODI 2026

📅 5 August 2026 📍 Sharjah Cricket Stadium ⏱️ 15:30 IST 🏏 Ireland tour of Afghanistan – 1st ODI

Afghanistan and Ireland lock horns in the first ODI of the series at the Sharjah Cricket Stadium. Both teams have a history of close contests, and with World Cup qualification points at stake, this promises to be a thrilling encounter.

Afghanistan – Squad & Form

Led by Hashmatullah Shahidi, Afghanistan are a spin-heavy side with world-class players like Rashid Khan and Mohammad Nabi. The batting revolves around Rahmanullah Gurbaz, Ibrahim Zadran, and Najibullah Zadran. Their pace attack is led by Fazalhaq Farooqi and Naveen-ul-Haq.

Ireland – Squad & Form

Ireland, captained by Andrew Balbirnie, are known for their fighting spirit. Paul Stirling remains the key batsman alongside Harry Tector and Curtis Campher. The bowling attack features the experienced Josh Little, Mark Adair, and the spin duo of George Dockrell and Ben White.

Pitch & Conditions

Sharjah offers a batting-friendly surface with some assistance for spinners as the match progresses. The weather is expected to be hot and dry, with temperatures around 38°C. The team winning the toss may prefer to field first to chase under lights.
